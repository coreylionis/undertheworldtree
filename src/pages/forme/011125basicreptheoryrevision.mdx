Let $G$ be a group. 
A **representation** of $G$ is a homomorphism 

<p style={{textAlign:`center`}}>$$\rho: G \to GL_n(V),$$</p>

where $V$ is a (finite dimensional) complex vector space. 

<u>Examples:</u>  
For any group $G$, we have the:
	- trivial representation $G \to GL_1(\mathbb{C}), \ g \mapsto 1$ 
	- regular representation $r_G : G \to GL_{|G|}(\mathbb{C}G), \ r_g(g)(e_h) = e_{gh}$ 
	- permutation representations: given a group action $G \curvearrowright X$, $p : G \to GL_{|X|}(\mathbb{C}X), \ p(g)(e_x) = e_{g\cdot x}$.
